Weight and aim report

Mean ± sample standard deviation. A single shot has no spread estimate. Fewer than five shots is marked insufficient; these are descriptive measurements, not validated player rankings. Both turns are combined within each matched call, direction and calibration. This reveals a consistent wide or narrow bias even when left/right errors cancel. Sweeping is reported as observed time.
